# Overview — What is hussh 🤫 One

## The one-liner

**hussh 🤫 One** is a single, secure, personal AI agent that lives across all your
surfaces — WhatsApp, terminal, dashboard — and acts on your behalf while keeping your
data, identity, and credentials private by construction.

## The name

- **hussh** — an acronym for **Hu**man **S**ecure **S**ocket **H**ost. A secure,
  human-centric hosting tunnel for your personal data and digital life.
- **🤫** — the quiet emoji. A deliberate double meaning:
  - the finger to the lips says *"shh!"* → confidentiality, privacy, secrets kept.
  - the single raised index finger visually forms the number **"One"**.
- **One** — your single unified cognitive companion. Not a dozen disconnected apps —
  one agent that coordinates everything securely.

Put together: **One** secure, human-centric agent that keeps your digital life
quiet, private, and yours — everywhere.

## What makes it different

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Privacy by construction | Capsule sandboxes, owner-only triggering, no data leakage between contexts |
| Present everywhere | Same agent on WhatsApp, terminal/TUI, and the web dashboard |
| Deterministic | Behavior is config-driven and test-backed — predictable, not emergent |
| Upgradeable | Built as an overlay on Hermes Agent; upstream updates never wipe the personality |
| Warm & human | Clean, friendly, jargon-free communication — never dumps logs or reasoning at people |

## Who it's for

- **The owner** (you) — full power across every surface, zero friction, just tag `@One`.
- **People you choose to share it with** — can interact with it inside sandboxed
  *capsules* (e.g. a friends group) without ever touching your private world.

## The north star

One day this agent is something anyone can adopt: a secure personal host that
represents them, remembers their context, and acts for them — without surrendering
their data to big tech. This documentation tree is how we build toward that,
one modular, deterministic, advertisable feature at a time.
